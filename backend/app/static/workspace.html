A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Your questions,
answered.

Get familiar with the process before you create your account.

Can I request documents without an account?

You can read about the service here without signing in. To link a record or submit an order, create an account and verify your email. Document recipients use their delivery link and email access code.

How much does a document request cost?

Each institution sets its service fees. You review a quote before giving consent and submitting your order. When payment is enabled, collection follows registrar approval.

How long will my request take?

Your institution lists a processing range for each service. Staff questions, holds, payment waits and deferred release conditions can affect when documents are ready. Planning targets are not guaranteed delivery dates.

What if my institution is not listed?

Open Institutions to browse active, approved universities and colleges without signing in. Ask your institution whether it participates. Demo institutions do not represent a real institution’s participation.

Who receives my documents?

You choose the recipients in your order and review them as part of your release consent. Where secure electronic delivery is configured, the designated recipient verifies their email with a single-use code.

ONE CONNECTED WORKFLOW

Built for the people
behind every record.

TranscriptsKE is an academic document request platform for students and institutions in Kenya. We are developing the workflow with demo institutions before expanding participation.

For students

Link your academic record, request available documents, review the quote and consent, and follow your order from your account.

For institutions

Authorized staff match records, review requests, manage holds, and prepare documents. Managers oversee the services and operational queues.

For recipients

When secure electronic delivery is enabled, use the institution’s complete delivery link and a single-use email code to download your document.

CARE AT EVERY STEP

Your information.
A considered process.

These are features of the application workflow. They are not a claim of accreditation, independent certification or participation by a particular institution.

Email verification and record ownership

Verifying your email confirms access to that mailbox. Institutional staff separately check your academic record ownership before allowing document requests.

Release consent and recipient access

You review the quote, documents and recipients before consenting to release. Configured electronic delivery uses expiring links and single-use recipient codes; institutions can revoke access.

Payments and service availability

Institutions set service fees and processing ranges. Card checkout or M-Pesa is available only when configured, and new orders are sent to the institution after confirmed checkout. Demo services do not imply a live provider connection.

WHEN YOU NEED A HAND

A little guidance
for your next step.

Find the right next action without sharing passwords, email codes or private records on social media.

I cannot sign in

Verify your email first. Use “Verify your email” on the sign-in screen to request another code, or “Forgot password?” to reset your password. Local development email mode saves messages in the configured mail directory.

I need an update on my request

Sign in and open Orders. Select your order to see its timeline, staff messages and any requested action. Your institution handles academic decisions and processing questions.

I received a document link

Open the complete link from the notification email. Enter the exact recipient email and request an access code. If the link is expired or revoked, contact the issuing institution.
